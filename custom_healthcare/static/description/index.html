What's inside the dashboard

Headline KPIs

Patients, appointments, attendance rate, consultations, bed occupancy and open CDS alerts — with trend vs the previous period. Click any tile to drill down.

Key Insights

Plain-language findings generated from live data: volume changes, no-show rate, largest care gaps, overloaded coordinators, bed pressure and abnormal labs.

Action Queues

Today's and overdue follow-ups, appointment requests, abnormal labs to review, open labs and referrals, care gaps and current inpatients — one click to the work list.

Clinic Activity

Appointment outcomes over time (completed, booked, no-show, cancelled) by day, week or month, and visit mix by appointment type.

Population Health

Risk stratification, programme compliance per chronic disease (on track / at risk / overdue), and age & gender demographics.

Clinical Quality

Lab results by test with abnormal rate, and the referral pipeline from sent to accepted, booked, feedback and closed.

Inpatient & Beds

Occupancy, current inpatients, average length of stay and estimated charges; bed status breakdown, ward occupancy and admissions vs discharges.

Care Coordination

Coordinator workload table (caseload, high-risk patients, open tasks, overdue share) and open CDS alerts by type.

Practice Benchmark

Side-by-side comparison of every practice: patients, appointments, attendance, no-show rate, high-risk share, overdue follow-ups and bed occupancy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about installation, compatibility, and clinical healthcare operations

Does Healthcare Operations Platform require external medical software or third-party subscriptions?
No. Healthcare Operations Platform runs 100% natively inside Odoo 19 (Community, Enterprise, and Odoo.sh), integrating directly with standard Contacts, Calendar, Invoicing, and Inventory without any external SaaS or subscription dependencies.
Which Odoo versions and editions are supported?
The module is fully tested and optimized for Odoo 19.0 Community, Enterprise, and Odoo.sh cloud deployments. All views and OWL dashboards adapt responsively to desktop and mobile screens.
How does role-based access protect sensitive patient data?
The module includes granular security groups (Healthcare Receptionist, Doctor, Nurse, Lab Technician, Coordinator, and Administrator) with strict record rules, ensuring unauthorized staff cannot view confidential clinical notes, diagnosis histories, or lab findings.
Can patients book appointments and view their prescriptions online?
Yes. The built-in Patient Portal allows patients to log in securely, check upcoming and past appointments, download verified lab reports, and review active medication prescriptions and dosages directly from their mobile or web browser.
Can we manage multiple clinics, branches, or hospital wards?
Yes. The system natively supports multi-practice architectures where appointments, doctors, consultation rooms, and inpatient wards can be partitioned per clinic branch or managed centrally across an entire healthcare group.
Is data migration from existing EHR or patient spreadsheets supported?
Standard Odoo CSV and Excel import facilities can be used to import patient records, medical histories, medications, and diagnosis codes with ease. Techvoot Solutions also provides migration assistance if required.
